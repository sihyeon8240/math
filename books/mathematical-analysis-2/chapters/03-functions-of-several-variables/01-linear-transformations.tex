This chapter assumes finite-dimensional linear algebra: bases, dimension,
linear maps and their matrix representations, rank--nullity, inner products,
orthogonalization, and norms. We also assume equivalence of norms in finite dimensions
and continuity of linear maps.

We write $A:\R^{n}\to\R^{m}$ for a linear map and use the same letter for its
matrix in the standard bases. Its rank is the dimension of its image.
Unless specified otherwise, vector norms are Euclidean. The operator norm is
\[
  \norm{A} = \sup_{\norm{x}\leq 1}\norm{Ax}.
\]
We use the standard estimate $\norm{Ax}\leq\norm{A}\norm{x}$.
Differentiability and the inverse, implicit, and rank theorems
will be developed in this chapter.
